Reports and Analysis
=====================

Regular and ad hoc reports are produced on selected viral and bacterial pathogens for use as references by the user community. If you use information obtained from these reports in publications or presentations, please be sure to cite the platform and publication. If you have ideas for other reports that would be helpful to the pathogen research community, please contact us with your suggestions through our HelpDesk.
